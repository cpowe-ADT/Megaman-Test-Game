# OMEGA RELAY v1.0 playtest checklist (EVAL-P8-009)

For Craig to tick while he plays the whole game, at `http://localhost:4180` (or a fresh `npm run build &&
npm run preview`). Play Classic on Normal unless a line says otherwise. Tick the box when that line's "what
good looks like" matches what you saw; leave it unticked and add a note otherwise. Results go in
`docs/prompts/DECISIONS.md` (if a call is needed) or straight to the next prompt's notes.

## Stages (route, mid-boss, secrets, capsule)

- [ ] **Drill Hangar (tutorial)** — the five teach locks open only on their verb, the wall-kick shaft climbs clean, a charged shot opens the saber-wall vault for the capsule, and the route ends at Rook's door.
- [ ] **Heat Works (Pyro Maw)** — the vent lane and slag climb read clearly, the catwalk room locks until the custodian walker falls, a charged shot opens the secret wall for the sub tank, and the capsule sits at the top of the climb.
- [ ] **Water District (Tide Reaver)** — the water level gates the sluices and the basin on a readable cycle, the intake room locks until the nest falls, high water floats you onto the sub-tank ledge, and the capsule waits in the rising shaft's alcove.
- [ ] **Power District (Volt Hopper)** — the rails and lane-swap platforms read on their beat, the rail room locks until the twins fall, a charged shot opens the secret wall for the sub tank, and the capsule sits on the bulkhead.
- [ ] **Structural Works (Basalt Titan)** — crumble slabs and rockfall telegraph before they drop, the shaft-head custodian walker's stomp reads by side, a charged shot opens the secret wall for the heart tank, and a timed dash jump reaches the sub tank.
- [ ] **Transit Security (Ferro Blade)** — the belts and bladed sweeps read their danger zone, the inspection-station nest locks the room until it falls, a charged shot opens the secret wall for the sub tank, and a dash jump off the wrong-way belt reaches the heart tank.
- [ ] **Medicine District (Mire Wraith)** — the acid pits and the switch-triggered rising acid read clearly, the serpent room locks until it falls, a charged shot opens the crate wall for the heart tank, and a dash jump in the filter tower reaches the sub tank.
- [ ] **Weather District (Gale Vixen)** — the gusts and ferries carry you across each gap on their beat, the twins' room locks until both fall, a charged shot opens the office wall for the sub tank, and the relay mast's gusts carry you to the capsule.
- [ ] **Public Archives (Glacier Ronin)** — ice shelves and falling icicles telegraph before they hurt, the custodian room locks until it falls, a charged shot opens the ice wall for the heart tank, and a dash jump off the cold store's ice reaches the sub tank.
- [ ] **Central Core (Omega Fortress, 3 acts)** — act 1 remixes familiar mechanics, act 2's hub opens all eight rematch doors (two refills on return) with the exit sealed until all eight fall, act 3 approaches the Core once the hub is empty, and the route holds together across all three acts.

- [ ] **Pyro Maw (Heat Works)** — the pre-stage card types "PYRO MAW" before the door, the fight telegraphs every attack and changes at phase two and desperation, a Hydro Lance hit staggers it, and the weapon-get card and demo show Flame Serpent's stream then its charged Inferno Coil.

- [ ] **Basalt Titan (Structural Works)** — the pre-stage card plays, the fight telegraphs every attack and changes at phase two and desperation, a Magcut Disc hit staggers it, and the weapon-get card and demo show Quake Knuckle's lob then its charged Fault Line.
- [ ] **Ferro Blade (Transit Security)** — the pre-stage card plays, the fight telegraphs every attack and changes at phase two and desperation, an Acid Glob hit staggers it, and the weapon-get card and demo show Magcut Disc's throw-and-return then its charged Twin Cutter.
- [ ] **Mire Wraith (Medicine District)** — the pre-stage card plays, the fight telegraphs every attack and changes at phase two and desperation, an Aero Darts hit staggers it, and the weapon-get card and demo show Acid Glob's stick-and-tick then its charged Corrosive Burst.

## Weapons (plain shot and its charged form)

- [ ] **Buster** — the starting weapon charges through four visible levels with no energy cost and never runs out.
- [ ] **Flame Serpent** — streams while held for a burning tag; held past full and released, it fires the charged Inferno Coil instead of a bigger plain shot.
- [ ] **Hydro Lance** — a tiltable piercing lance; charged, it fires Tidal Surge, which pierces several foes in a line.
- [ ] **Thunder Spike** — arcs to one nearby foe; charged, Storm Burst chains to up to five foes in a radius.
- [ ] **Quake Knuckle** — a lobbed knuckle that quakes the floor; charged, Fault Line sends the shockwave both ways.
- [ ] **Magcut Disc** — thrown out and back, pulling drops in; charged, Twin Cutter pierces more foes on the way.
- [ ] **Acid Glob** — sticks and ticks damage; charged, Corrosive Burst ticks more times for more total damage.
- [ ] **Aero Darts** — three bouncing darts in a spread; charged, Cyclone Volley fires five in a wider spread.
- [ ] **Frost Shatter** — freezes a foe briefly; charged, Glacial Ram freezes for about 2.5s and pierces.
- [ ] Every special's bar reads 28 units and empties at the rate its per-shot cost (1, 2 or 4) implies; a charged shot always costs double and never fires for free.

## Beats (READY, results, debrief, game over, the ending record)

- [ ] **READY** — blinks three times over about 1.2s before control at a stage's start, and blinks once (600ms, no card) after a mid-stage respawn.
- [ ] **Stage results** — time, secrets found, lives used and the active difficulty show after a boss falls, before the weapon-get card.
- [ ] **District return debrief** — after every warden clear, a skippable 2-to-3-line exchange between Iona and the hero plays on the way back to Stage Select, before any milestone line.
- [ ] **Game over** — OMEGA's line shows on the first game over and Iona's on the second, over the Continue row, and the active run resumes from the last claimed checkpoint.
- [ ] **Ending record** — the `CAMPAIGN RECORD` shows a rank (0 deaths FLAWLESS, 1 to 9 STEADY, 10 or more RELENTLESS), the credits hold each line at least 2.5s, and the `OMEGA RELAY` title card closes it.

## Menus and options

- [ ] **Title** — the logo, loading screen and attract-mode loop play before Press Start; the attract loop cycles a stage preview.
- [ ] **New Campaign** — name entry, then Assist/Normal/Veteran, create the save with no leftover state from a cancelled attempt.
- [ ] **Stage Select** — all nine tiles (the eight wardens plus the Central Core) show their district preview, flip to a description and reward preview, and the cursor reads clearly at both render scales.
- [ ] **Pause / System Menu** — weapon select, sub tank use, Options and quit all work from pause, and resuming returns control exactly where it paused.
- [ ] **Options** — Fullscreen, Pixel scaling (Smooth/Integer), Reduced Flashing and Touch Controls (Auto/On/Off) all take effect immediately and survive a reload.
- [ ] **Controls** — keyboard and gamepad bindings remap, a conflict swaps the other binding instead of erroring, and Reset restores the defaults.
