## Outcome of this prompt

Ten stages at the Heat Works standard, twelve mechanics, four mini-boss archetypes, ten boss fights that telegraph, change by phase and present their intro and death, every weapon with its own shot, a story that speaks at every beat, a sound for every action and a track per stage, and a v1.0 build on `main` that CI proves and Craig has played end to end.

## How the parts run

- At most three implementation lanes at once (the machine runs smoke and sweep for each). One writer per file set; each task card names the files a lane owns. `src/scenes/Game.ts` belongs to one lane at a time and only shrinks.
- Shared files (`TESTING.md`, `scripts/smoke-test.mjs`, `assets/sprites/manifest.v1.json`, `src/content/stages/index.ts`) take one small insertion, re-read right before editing.
- Art comes from Higgsfield (`gpt_image_2`, cut with `scripts/sprites/cut_vfx_sheet.py`, `cut_enemy_sheet.py`, `cut_tileset.py`), recorded in a `.prompts.md` and `free-source-attribution.v1.json` before runtime use. Higgsfield has no general music or sound-effect model (its audio tool says so): audio is generated by scripts in the repo or taken from CC0 sources with Craig's permission per download.
- Every lane ends with its gates pasted, the captures it cites opened and described, and a result card; the orchestrator verifies on the shared tree (a snapshot copy when lanes are running), commits, and writes the ledger rows.
- Waves: wave 1 is 12a, 12b and 12f (7.0 extraction then telegraphs); wave 2 is 12c, 12f (phase kits, weapons) and 12g; then 12d in batches as each stage's mechanics land, 12h beside it, 12e, and 12i last.
