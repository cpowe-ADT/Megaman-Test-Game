- Weapon-get shows only the control hint from `roster.ts`.
- Game over is silent while the antagonist narrates every stage.

- Iona's turn ("I am done defending the design") lands on a defence the player never heard; she is suspicious from the prologue.
- OMEGA speaks and never acts; every intrusion is a ticker line with no consequence in the level.
- The ending pays off eight districts but the briefing numbers (3,000; 6,000; two days) never return; the Drill Hangar and Rook get no card; Iona's want to be forgiven has no beat.
- No narrative reason to replay; 8/8 capsules changes nothing on screen.

- Plot hole B: wardens are "linked by consent" after being beaten; the theme is coordination without coercion. OMEGA never lays the charge, so WREN's refusal answers nothing.
- The Core radio and Core defeat repeat "fail differently"; the last stage is "Central Core", "Omega Fortress" and "Omega Citadel Command Vault" in three places.
